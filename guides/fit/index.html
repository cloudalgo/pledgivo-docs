Fit checklist

Is Pledgivo a fit?

Five questions about your org. Each answer shows what the package supports today, in plain words. There is no score: it is a list of facts to check against your own setup.

1Which Salesforce edition do you run?
Enterprise
Supported, including public donation pages on Experience Cloud.
Unlimited
Supported, including public donation pages on Experience Cloud.
Performance
Supported, including public donation pages on Experience Cloud.
Professional
Supported for internal use only: Experience Cloud, which public donation pages need, is not available on Professional.
Something else
Developer edition is supported. For any other edition, tell us which and we will check.
2Contacts and Accounts, or Person Accounts?
Contacts and Accounts
Supported; the package detects which your org uses.
Person Accounts
Supported; the package detects which your org uses.
3Do you use NPSP?
Yes
Supported alongside NPSP; an optional sync copies recurring gifts and designations into NPSP's own objects.
No
Not needed.
4Where are you, and can you use Stripe?
United States, with Stripe
Supported.
United Kingdom, with Stripe
Supported.
Somewhere else
Pledgivo supports the US and the UK today.
We cannot use Stripe
Pledgivo takes card payments through Stripe only.
5Roughly how much do you raise online a year?
Under $50,000
Use the estimator on the home page to compare costs.
$50,000 to $250,000
Use the estimator on the home page to compare costs.
$250,000 to $1 million
Use the estimator on the home page to compare costs.
Over $1 million
Use the estimator on the home page to compare costs.
