    Pledgivo Documentation

    Run fundraising natively on Salesforce#

    Pledgivo is a managed package for donation processing, recurring giving, campaigns, events, and peer-to-peer fundraising — installed directly into your org, with donor data that never leaves it.

    Most fundraising platforms ask you to run donor data through an external system and sync it back to Salesforce on a schedule. Pledgivo skips that step. The donation form, the payment processing, the recurring billing, and the donor records all live on the same Salesforce instance as your Contacts, Accounts, and Opportunities — queryable the moment a gift lands, reportable with standard Salesforce reports, and governed by your org's existing sharing model.

    2GP managed package Namespace: pledgivo API 66.0 Enhanced LWR Stripe payments

    What it does#

    • Donations & payments

      A public, guest-accessible donation form backed by Stripe's Payment Element — card and ACH, one-time or recurring — with donation records landing directly on the standard Opportunity object.

      Learn about donations & payments →

    • Recurring giving

      Salesforce owns the billing schedule and charges the donor's saved card off-session on its own cadence, with built-in dunning for failed payments — no external subscription system to reconcile.

      Learn about recurring giving →

    • Campaigns

      Every fundraising ask is a standard Salesforce Campaign, themed with a reusable page design and composed from movable content and field blocks — no page-builder platform bolted on the side.

      Learn about campaigns →

    • Events & peer-to-peer

      Ticketed events with tiered pricing and seat capacity, and peer-to-peer fundraising pages and teams for donor-led campaigns — both built on the same donation pipeline.

      Learn about events & ticketing →

    Why native matters#

    Typical external platform Pledgivo
    Donor data Synced back to Salesforce on a schedule Written directly to Salesforce, no sync job
    Payment confirmation Inbound webhook to a public endpoint Salesforce re-fetches payment status from Stripe — no webhook to secure
    Reporting Requires a connector or export Standard Salesforce reports and dashboards
    Sharing & security A second permission model to maintain Your org's existing sharing rules and permission sets
    Person Accounts Usually unsupported or bolted on Detected at runtime, supported throughout
