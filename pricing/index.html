    03

    Where a donation actually goes

    Card to your bank account. We are not a stop along the way.

    Donor gives Card details go from the donor's browser straight to Stripe. They never reach your org.
    Stripe processes Stripe takes its processing fee and settles the rest, on your own Stripe account.
    Your bank The balance lands in your account, and the donation is already a record in your org.
    Pledgivo — $0 or $299 a month A flat subscription billed to you like any other software. It is never deducted from a gift.

    Which is why the price is identical whether you raise $5,000 or $5,000,000. The only thing that moves you off the free plan is the number of donations you take, never their size.

    05

    Questions worth asking first

    Particularly the one about reaching 200 donations mid-campaign.

    What counts as a donation?

    One completed gift — a single donation, one charge of a recurring schedule, or one ticket order. Refunded and failed payments do not count towards the free plan's 200.

    Is the free plan a trial?

    No. It does not expire, it does not ask for a card, and it is not a cut-down edition — it is the complete package running on your own org, with a 200-donation-a-year ceiling and a Powered by Pledgivo line on your donor pages.

    What happens when we reach 200?

    Campaigns already running keep taking donations. Nothing is switched off mid-appeal and no gift is ever refused — the thing we would be interrupting is the thing you built the campaign for. What the ceiling governs is starting a new campaign. We get in touch as you approach it and move you across before it bites.

    What is the "Powered by Pledgivo" line?

    One line of text at the foot of your public donation pages on the free plan, linking back to us. On the paid plan it is not there and your donor pages carry your branding only.

    Is it priced per user?

    No. The subscription covers a Salesforce org and every user in it can be given access. Adding staff never changes the bill.

    Do you take a cut of donations?

    No, and structurally we cannot. The package runs inside your own org and Stripe settles to your own Stripe account — there is no point at which the money passes through us.

    Can we use more than one Stripe account?

    Yes. Connect as many as you need — a second legal entity, a fiscal sponsee, a restricted programme — and point each campaign at exactly one of them. Every account carries its own credentials, and test accounts stay separate from live ones.

    How do we find out why a gift failed?

    A Diagnostic Logs tab inside the app shows what the package recorded about its own behaviour, filtered by level, context and date. A health check grades the org's configuration, and you choose how many days of logs to keep before a nightly job deletes them.

    Which Salesforce editions work?

    Enterprise, Unlimited and Performance. Professional edition can run the internal features but not the public donor site, which needs Experience Cloud.

    What happens if we cancel?

    Your data stays. Campaigns, donations and contacts are ordinary Salesforce records in your own org — uninstalling the package does not take them with it.
