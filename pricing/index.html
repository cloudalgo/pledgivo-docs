Questions

Questions worth asking first.

Particularly the one about who actually needs a licence.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — 5 days, with every feature and unlimited donations, from the day you install. A badge at the top of each staff console counts the days down, and its Complete checkout button takes you to the subscription portal when you are ready. If the trial ends first, the staff consoles close until you subscribe; your donation pages, receipts and donor portal keep working, and your data is untouched. How the trial works.

Who needs a licence?

Only fundraising admins — the staff who build campaigns, manage donations and configure the app inside your org. Three is the minimum, and most organisations need no more than a handful.

Do donors or event attendees need one?

Never. Giving, registering for an event, and using the donor portal all happen on your public pages — no licence, no login to your org, no effect on your bill.

Is there a limit on donations?

No. Every licence includes unlimited donations, campaigns and events — the bill moves with the size of your team, never with your fundraising results.

Why a three-licence minimum?

It sets the floor of the subscription: every org starts at $147 a month, or $1,470 a year. You can hold more licences than you have admins today and grow into them.

How does annual billing work?

$490 a user a year instead of $49 a month — twelve months for the price of ten, about $41 a user a month. The plan is otherwise identical.

Do you take a cut of donations?

No, and structurally we cannot. The package runs inside your own org and Stripe settles to your own Stripe account — there is no point at which the money passes through us.

Can we use more than one Stripe account?

Yes. Connect as many as you need — a second legal entity, a fiscal sponsee, a restricted programme — and point each campaign at exactly one of them. Every account carries its own credentials, and test accounts stay separate from live ones.

How do we find out why a gift failed?

A Diagnostic Logs tab inside the app shows what the package recorded about its own behaviour, filtered by level, context and date. A health check grades the org's configuration, and you choose how many days of logs to keep before a nightly job deletes them.

Which Salesforce editions work?

Enterprise, Unlimited and Performance. Professional edition can run the internal features but not the public donor site, which needs Experience Cloud.

What happens if we cancel?

Your data stays. Campaigns, donations and contacts are ordinary Salesforce records in your own org — uninstalling the package does not take them with it.
